Powerful Features

Designed for modern student workflows

Ditch the messy folders and disjointed tools. NoteBase centralizes your entire study cycle in one responsive web workspace.

Module Workspace

Organize your academic syllabus into clean, color-coded Modules. Rearrange modules via intuitive drag-and-drop handles and track note counts in real-time.

  • Optimistic UI for instant updates
  • Drag & Drop module sorting
  • Quick file and note count indicators

Notion-Style Editor

Author clean markdown notes with premium cover gradients and custom emoji icons. Edit content with heading hierarchies, lists, color highlights, and inline attachments.

  • Automatic 1.5s debounce autosave
  • Notion-like cover picking & emoji select
  • In-note image and PDF attachments

Document Vaults

Upload PDF lecture notes, study slides, and past papers (organized by academic year). Sync files to Firebase Storage and Google Drive directly from your dashboard.

  • Drag & drop PDF uploads (up to 5 MB)
  • Organize past papers by year
  • Real-time storage limit monitoring

Focus Planner

Supercharge your revision sessions using the integrated Pomodoro focus timer. Manage today's task checklist, track deadlines, and visualize your weekly study progress charts.

  • Focus, Short Break, Long Break states
  • Daily interactive task list manager
  • Upcoming deadlines & weekly study analytics

NoteBase AI Tutor

Chat directly with your uploaded notes. Ask the AI to summarize complex concepts, generate custom practice MCQ quizzes, outline study plans, or solve tricky math equations.

  • Instant PDF document summarization
  • Auto-generated practice MCQ quizzes
  • Renders mathematical LaTeX formulas

Batchmate Community

Connect with classmates by sharing study sheets and resource links. Discover highly rated peer files on the trending leaderboard and toggling profile visibility.

  • Public vault sharing & community feed
  • Trending notes leaderboard rankings
  • Granular privacy settings (Private vs Public)

Under the Hood

Engineered for performance & reliability

NoteBase leverages modern cloud technologies to ensure your notes are backed up, encrypted, and accessible instantly.

Clerk Auth

Industrial-grade authentication ensuring only you can access your notes and database. Supports rapid email signups.

Google Drive API

Integrates with Google Drive storage to upload past paper PDFs and images. Your study guides are synced reliably.

Firebase Storage

Powers rapid assets loading and note attachments hosting so files open instantly when revising in the library.

SWR Caching

Data is pre-fetched and updated optimistically in the background, making the UI feel fast and responsive.
